The Treasure-Hunting Mission

Searching for an optimal solution is like hunting for pirate treasure on an island. You and your friends are the search agents, and every part of the book gives you a different way to search. Pick a hunting strategy, press Run, and watch it work on the same island.

Based on Alaa Khamis, Optimization Algorithms: AI techniques for design, planning, and control problems, Manning, 2024.

Fire Mountain is the highest point on the island, but it is an active volcano, so it can never be the answer. This is why constraints matter: the best feasible solution is not always the highest point overall.
